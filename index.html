Expert and highly experienced researcher and quantitative methodologist with 15+ years of demonstrated success specializing in measurement science and research methods. At Amazon Web Services, I led end-to-end analytical and machine learning initiatives that shape product decisions, automate data pipelines, and improve operational efficiency. I served as the certification psychometrician for multiple AWS certification exams. My work bridges deep measurement and statistical expertise with scalable Python, R, SQL, and AWS infrastructure.

Before AWS, I was a tenured Associate Professor of Statistics, Measurement, and Evaluation in Education at the University of Missouri (2008–2022). My academic research focused on statistical modeling using large-scale assessment data, measurement, scale development, and program evaluation. I co-developed the Classroom Engagement Inventory and authored 50+ peer-reviewed papers, three books, and six book chapters.

Books

Two open-access books I’ve written for graduate-level quantitative methods courses.

Quantitative Foundations in Educational Research

A self-contained introduction to applied statistics for educational and social-science research: descriptive statistics, probability, sampling, inference, regression, and ANOVA. Developed for ESC_PS 8850, the master’s-level methods course I taught at the University of Missouri. Previous site → · Lecture slides →

Structural Equation Modeling Using R and Mplus

A practical introduction to structural equation modeling with worked examples in both R (lavaan) and Mplus. Covers measurement models, path analysis, latent variable models, model fit, and multi-group / multilevel extensions. Built around ESC_PS 9710, a doctoral course I taught at the University of Missouri. Previous site → · Lecture slides →

NSF-funded collaborative research project, Should I Stay or Should I Go? Understanding the Retention of Latinx in Engineering Jobs. Award: NSF 2000607, $1,546,428 (2020–2025). PIs: Lisa Y. Flores (University of Missouri), Rachel L. Navarro (University of North Dakota). Role: Key Personnel.
